About Wordle

Wordle is a free daily word puzzle where you guess a hidden 5-letter word in 6 tries. After each guess, tiles change color to show how close you are. Play the daily puzzle once per day, or practice unlimited in free mode.

Features

  • Daily puzzle — one word per day, same for everyone
  • Free practice mode — unlimited random words
  • Color feedback: green, yellow, gray
  • On-screen keyboard + physical keyboard support
  • Win/loss statistics and streak tracking
  • Share results as emoji blocks — no spoilers
  • Works on desktop and mobile
  • 100% free, no signup, no download

How Colors Work

Green — the letter is in the word and in the correct spot. Yellow — the letter is in the word but in the wrong spot. Gray — the letter is not in the word at all.
